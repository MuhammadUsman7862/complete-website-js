Testing Notes - Task 10 (Responsive CSS Project)

DRAFT: I wrote these from the CSS. Before submitting, open the page in DevTools,
check each line, and change anything that does not match what you really see.

Mobile (375px, device toolbar)
What I saw: header wraps (brand, nav, theme switch). Hero is one column. Cards are 1 column.
Form fields are 1 column. Footer is 1 column. The table scrolls inside its box. No sideways page scroll.
Task cards stay stacked (their container is narrower than 22rem).

Tablet (768px)
What I saw: About is 2 columns (3fr / 2fr). Form groups are 2 columns. Cards show 2-3 per row.
Task cards switch to text-left, badge-right because the container query matches.

Desktop (1280px)
What I saw: hero is 2 columns, footer is 3 columns (2fr 1fr 1fr), content is limited to 80rem.

Theme
What I saw: Auto follows the system. Light and Dark both work from either system setting.

Focus states (Tab key)
What I saw: nav links, buttons, FAQ summaries and form fields show a clear outline. Skip link appears first.

Form states
What I saw: required fields show *, invalid fields get a red border and "Please check this field.",
valid fields get a green border and "Looks good.", the Remote option is greyed out (disabled).

Reduced motion (DevTools > Rendering > Emulate prefers-reduced-motion)
What I saw: hero entry animation, badge pulse, hover lifts and smooth scroll all stop.

Print preview (Ctrl+P)
What I saw: header, theme switch, buttons, video, audio, map and image are hidden. Colours are black on white.
External link addresses are printed after the link text.

Computed styles
What I saw: in the Computed tab the colours come from the --primary and --surface variables, and change when the theme changes.
